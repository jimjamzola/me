Research Focus

I am an applied mathematician working on the analysis, control, and learning of nonlinear waves and dynamical systems, mostly in Hamiltonian, quantum, and optical settings. Much of my recent work builds data-driven methods that find mathematical structure, such as total or near integrability, in simulation data. My earlier work, which I still very much enjoy, is in optimal control theory and nonlinear waves.

New and Active Projects (please come to my talks if not published yet)

  • Reduced Order Modeling via spectral methods
  • Neural methods for long-time integration of PDEs
  • Applied analysis of nonlinear waves, lately working on the Manakov system and the Salerno lattice
  • Learning structure (integrability, integral transforms, conservation laws, normal forms, etc.) from data
  • Data-driven methods in open quantum systems
